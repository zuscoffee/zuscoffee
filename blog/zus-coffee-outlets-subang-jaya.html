Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best ZUS Coffee outlet in Subang Jaya?

It depends on what you want. For affordable grab-and-go, ZUS Coffee Ngupi @ Restoran Pak Haji Ali in USJ 8 is the top pick — RM3.90 flat per cup, dispensed fresh. For a premium café experience, Signature branches like Sunway Pyramid and Jalan Puteri are excellent.

Why is ZUS Coffee Ngupi @ Restoran Pak Haji Ali so popular?

It completely redefines the café experience by introducing an ultra-affordable dispenser format: a completely flat price of RM3.90 per cup, machine-dispensed Iced White Coffee and Iced Chocolate Coffee, and a casual, community-driven vibe inside Restoran Pak Haji Ali in USJ 8.

How much is coffee at ZUS Coffee Ngupi?

A completely flat price of RM3.90 per cup. No hidden charges, no size upgrades, no service charge. Two cups cost RM7.80, five cups cost RM19.50, ten cups cost RM39.

What is the difference between ZUS Coffee Ngupi and ZUS Signature Coffee?

ZUS Coffee Ngupi @ Restoran Pak Haji Ali offers a fast, budget-friendly dispenser format at RM3.90 flat per cup with a two-drink menu. ZUS Signature Coffee branches offer a premium crafted menu with wider selections, custom sweetness, and comfortable café seating at standard pricing.

Is there a ZUS Coffee in SS15 Subang Jaya?

Yes. ZUS Coffee SS15 is a standard outlet rated 4.4 with 183 reviews. It's known for being laptop-friendly, student-popular, well air-conditioned, and open until midnight — later than most mall branches.

Which ZUS outlet in Subang Jaya has the best seating for working?

ZUS Signature Coffee at Sunway Pyramid LG (Oasis) and ZUS Coffee SS15 are the best options for working. Sunway Pyramid has an upscale, work-friendly indoor-outdoor setup; SS15 is cozy, student-friendly, and open until midnight.

Where can I find ZUS Coffee in Easyhome Mall?

ZUS Coffee Easyhome Mall is at G-25, Easyhome Mall, Persiaran Kewajipan, Subang Jaya. It's rated 4.4 with 136 reviews and is known for accurate drink measurements, quiet atmosphere, and ample free parking.

Can I get custom sweetness at ZUS Coffee Ngupi?

No. Sweetness levels are fixed at ZUS Coffee Ngupi, and drinks are served exclusively ice-cold. This guarantees consistent speed and flavour every time you visit. For custom sweetness, choose a Signature branch.

What food pairs well with ZUS Coffee Ngupi?

Because Ngupi sits inside Restoran Pak Haji Ali, it pairs flawlessly with the restaurant's local staples — especially crispy fried chicken. A coffee run can easily become a full meal.
