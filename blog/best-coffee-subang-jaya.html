🧊 Why Ice-Cold Coffee Works

Iced coffee isn't just for hot days (though in Malaysia, that's most days). Serving coffee ice-cold:

  • Locks in the flavour — serving over ice preserves a smoother, less acidic taste.
  • Is more refreshing — perfect for Malaysia's tropical climate.
  • Pairs better with food — especially with the savoury dishes at Restoran Pak Haji Ali.
  • Is easier to drink on the go — no waiting for it to cool down.
