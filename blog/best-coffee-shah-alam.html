1. Shah Alam's Coffee Scene in 2026

Shah Alam is not short on cafes. The city has quietly built one of the more interesting coffee scenes in Selangor — Kota Kemuning alone has a cluster of independent roasteries and specialty shops, while Seksyen 13, Seksyen 7, and Setia Alam each have their own growing list of spots.

But there is a catch: Shah Alam cafes are priced like the neighbourhoods they sit in. A flat white runs RM13 to RM16. An iced latte with oat milk can hit RM18. A brunch-and-coffee combo easily clears RM40 per person.

That is fine if you are going for the atmosphere. But for the everyday need — a good, cold coffee at a price you do not have to think about — Shah Alam has a real gap. And ZUS Coffee Ngupi is exactly what fills it.
