1. The Honest Truth About Coffee in Puchong

Puchong has no shortage of cafes. Bandar Puteri alone has enough brunch spots, bubble tea shops, and third-wave coffee bars to fill a weekend. IOI Mall has a handful more. Puchong Jaya has its own little cafe strip.

But here is the problem nobody talks about: almost every one of those cafes charges RM10 to RM18 for an iced coffee. Some reach RM20 once you add a syrup or a size upgrade. A regular Puchong cafe run — one drink, one snack — easily clears RM25 per person.

That is fine if you are going for the atmosphere. But if you just want a good, cold, no-nonsense coffee at a price you do not have to think about, Puchong does not really have an answer.

ZUS Coffee Ngupi does. It is 15 minutes away in Subang Jaya, and it charges RM3.90 per cup, flat.
